\begin{center}\bfseries ABSTRAK\end{center}
\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent Struktur Kurikulum Merdeka yang memisahkan rumpun Ilmu Pengetahuan
Alam pada jenjang SMA menjadi Fisika, Kimia, dan Biologi berisiko menimbulkan
\textit{siloed learning}, yakni terputusnya koneksi konseptual antarmata
pelajaran. Penelitian ini mengembangkan \textit{pipeline}
\textit{LLM-Assisted Knowledge Graph Completion} (KGC) untuk membangun dan
melengkapi graf pengetahuan sains lintas-disiplin Fase~F Kelas~XII berbahasa
Indonesia berbasis buku teks resmi Kemendikbudristek. Mengikuti kerangka
\textit{Design Science Research}, sistem mengekstraksi entitas dan relasi
intra-buku menggunakan \textit{Large Language Model} (LLM) dengan
\textit{prompt few-shot} terpandu skema ontologi OWL, menginstansiasinya ke
basis data graf Neo4j, lalu memprediksi relasi implisit lintas-disiplin melalui
pencarian kandidat \textit{Approximate Nearest Neighbor} (HNSW) atas
\textit{embedding} deskripsi konsep yang dilanjutkan klasifikasi relasi oleh
LLM. Kualitas graf dievaluasi secara \textit{hybrid} melalui metrik struktural
dan validasi enam pakar domain, dengan \textit{LLM-as-a-judge} sebagai penengah
ketidaksepakatan. Penambahan 125 relasi lintas-buku menurunkan
\textit{Modularity} dari 0{,}811 menjadi 0{,}373 dan menaikkan
\textit{Average Degree Centrality} sebesar 98{,}4\%, menunjukkan ketiga
komunitas mata pelajaran yang semula terisolasi berhasil dijembatani. Validasi
pakar menunjukkan ekstraksi intra-mapel tergolong baik (F1-Score 92{,}6\%)
dan relasi lintas-disiplin valid (99{,}1\%) serta tepat tipe (98{,}3\%),
namun ketepatan arah relasi hanya 75{,}9\% akibat kesalahan sistematis pada
pasangan Kimia--Biologi, dengan Kimia berperan sebagai poros yang menghubungkan
kedua disiplin lain. Penelitian ini menunjukkan LLM-Assisted KGC layak untuk
pemetaan kurikulum sains lintas-disiplin dan menghasilkan purwarupa graf
pengetahuan sains lintas-disiplin berbahasa Indonesia yang terbuka, dengan
catatan perbaikan ketepatan arah relasi masih diperlukan.
\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent\textbf{Kata kunci:} Knowledge Graph Completion, Large Language Models
(LLM), LLM-as-a-judge, Kurikulum Merdeka, Pendidikan Sains Lintas-Disiplin,
Pemetaan Kurikulum.

\begin{center}\bfseries ABSTRACT\end{center}
\vspace{0.6cm}
\setstretch{1.4}
\frow{Name}{Fadrian Yhoga Pratama, Soros Febriano, Tegar Wahyu Khisbulloh}
\frow{Student ID}{2206819395, 2206083445, 2206082032}
\frow{Study Program}{Computer Science}
\frow{Title}{LLM-Assisted Knowledge Graph Completion for Mapping the
Interconnections of Science Topics (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) in the Grade XII
Senior High School Merdeka Curriculum}
\frow{Counsellor 1}{Dr.\ Siti Aminah, S.Kom., M.Kom.}
\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent The Merdeka Curriculum structure, which separates the senior high
school natural sciences into Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, risks inducing
\textit{siloed learning}, namely the loss of conceptual connections across
subjects. This study develops an \textit{LLM-Assisted Knowledge Graph
Completion} (KGC) \textit{pipeline} to construct and complete a cross-disciplinary
Indonesian-language science knowledge graph for Phase~F (Grade~XII), based on
the official Kemendikbudristek textbooks. Following the \textit{Design Science
Research} framework, the system extracts intra-book entities and relations using
a \textit{Large Language Model} (LLM) with schema-constrained, ontology-guided
\textit{few-shot} prompting, instantiates them into a Neo4j graph database, and
then predicts implicit cross-disciplinary relations through
\textit{Approximate Nearest Neighbor} (HNSW) candidate retrieval over concept
description \textit{embeddings}, followed by LLM relation classification. Graph
quality is evaluated using a hybrid approach combining structural metrics and
validation by six domain experts, with an \textit{LLM-as-a-judge} mediating
disagreements. Adding 125 cross-book relations lowered \textit{Modularity} from
0.811 to 0.373 and raised \textit{Average Degree Centrality} by 98.4\%,
indicating that the three initially isolated subject communities were bridged.
Expert validation showed good intra-subject extraction (F1-Score 92.6\%) and
valid cross-disciplinary relations (99.1\%) with accurate typing (98.3\%), but
relation-direction accuracy reached only 75.9\%, owing to a systematic error on
Chemistry--Biology pairs, with Chemistry acting as the hub connecting the other
two disciplines. This study shows that LLM-Assisted KGC is viable for
cross-disciplinary science curriculum mapping and produces an open Indonesian
cross-disciplinary science knowledge-graph prototype, with the caveat that
relation-direction accuracy still requires improvement.
\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Knowledge Graph Completion, Large Language Models
(LLM), LLM-as-a-judge, Merdeka Curriculum, Cross-Disciplinary Science Education,
Curriculum Mapping.
